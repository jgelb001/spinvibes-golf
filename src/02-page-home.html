The One Big Idea 🌺
Ball First, Ground Second
When you swing an iron, the club should touch the ball first — then brush the ground just after. If the club hits the ground before the ball, you'll hear a thud and the ball will barely move. That's the most common beginner mistake. Keep reminding yourself: ball first, ground second.
The ground comes second. Every time.

Goals 🌺
🎯 Make clean contact with the ball

😊 Keep it fun — every solid hit is a win
📈 Build the habit before worrying about distance
The Mantra: Contact first, always. Everything else comes with time.
Session Tips 🌱
⏱ 20–30 minutes is plenty — stop before it stops being fun
🌺 Always start with the 9-iron — warm into the swing
🎵 One swing thought per session — never change mid-session
🤲 Light grip pressure — firm but not crushing, like holding toothpaste
🏆 Celebrate every clean hit — that's the whole point right now
