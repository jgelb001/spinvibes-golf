Break 90 · Game Plan
Course Strategy
One thought per shot. Trust the process.
Tee Box
Pick a specific target, a tree, a bush, or an edge of fairway. Never aim at "out there."
Driver: tee low, ball forward, sweep up. One swing thought max.
If driver isn't working → 4H or 7W off the tee. Take your medicine early.
Fairway & Approach
From 150+ yds: play for the middle of the green. No hero shots.
From 100 yds and in: pick a specific landing spot, not just the flag.
Trouble left? Aim right. Trouble right? Aim left. Always have an out.
Uneven lie: choke down 1–2 inches. Smooth and balanced beats hard and tall.
Around the Green
Bump-and-run first. Use 7i, PW, or GW, land it on the green and let it run.
Only use SW when you need to carry something, bunker, fringe, tight lie.
Weight forward, hands ahead, brush the grass. It's a long putt with a lofted club.
Putting
3-footers: eyes over ball, pendulum, listen for the rattle.
Long putts: focus on speed first. Get it within 3 feet, avoid 3-putts.
Read break from below the hole. Aim for a spot 6 inches in front of the ball.
Mental
One shot at a time. Bad shot → deep breath → let it go before the next swing.
Bogey is fine. Double bogey happens. Move on, the round isn't over.
Never change your swing mid-round. Range is for experimenting. Course is for committing.
The 3 Rules That Save Shots
1. Never attempt a shot you haven't practiced. The course is not the place to try something for the first time.
2. Plan your miss before you swing. Know which side of the trouble is the easy side. Aim away from the hole that costs you.
3. Breathe on the first tee, last hole, and anything over water. That's where adrenaline eats strokes. Slow down exactly there.
