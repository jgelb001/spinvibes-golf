One app, the whole family

A six-year-old and a grandparent, both in the game.

Every member gets their own profile and their own plan, keyed to their age, hand and goal. A kid learning to swing and a parent chasing a lower score see completely different apps, under one family. Switch between them with a tap.

You, personally

Live in the app now
Your custom plan — range, short game and strategy for your skill, goal and hand.
AI caddie — knows your bag and misses; ask club and strategy anytime, even mid-hole, by typing or talking.
Round tracking — hole-by-hole scores, approach distances, hole photos and notes.
Scan a scorecard — snap a photo and it fills the course, pars and yardages.
Log and edit rounds — past rounds, practice sessions, and edit any scorecard.
Handicap and stats — a running handicap and a shot-dispersion read on your misses.
Badges and memories — achievement badges and a photo gallery of your rounds.
Share cards — scorecard, hole highlight, best moment, family recap.
Built for the course — stays awake, PIN lock, light and dark, magic-link sign-in, works offline.

The whole family

Live in the app now
A profile per member — each with their own plan; age-gated kid voice for 6–8 and 9–12.
Switch profiles — pills up top; add, edit or remove family in Settings.
Family round — everyone scored on one phone, combined recap and a shareable card.
All seven game modes — Just for fun, Junior Scramble, Parents vs. Kids, Team Scramble, Hole Challenge, Kid Birdie Bomb, Team Captain.
Kids level up — points and badges across 9 levels (Fresh Caddie → Tour Bound), per-level challenges, daily check-in.
Family stats sync — every member's rounds follow the family across devices.
